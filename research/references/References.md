## F. How the References Are Used
- Problem Statement — user/trust problems and their relevance.
- User Research — secondary evidence and primary-research questions.
- Competitor Analysis — documented competitor capabilities.
- Market Research — market size, growth and digital-travel context.
- Solution Research — evidence-to-solution and technical approach.

## G. Source Handling Rules
- Prefer first-party or official government sources for factual claims.
- Clearly label commercial market-research estimates as estimates.
- Do not claim a competitor lacks a feature without reliable evidence.
- Keep source URLs with relevant research documents.
- Replace major claims with newer evidence when appropriate.
