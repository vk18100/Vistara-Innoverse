# Vistara - INNOVERSE Problem Statement

## 1. Problem Context
Travelers need more than a list of stays. They need a stay that fits their needs, confidence that the property can be trusted, useful information from reviews, and a connected way to discover the destination.

## 2. Core User Problems
1. **Choosing a suitable stay** — many listings can satisfy individual filters while still being a poor overall match.
2. **Trusting the property** — users need stronger confidence that a listing represents a real and appropriate stay.
3. **Understanding reviews** — important details can be buried in free text.
4. **Discovering the destination** — users may still need separate searches for places, food, experiences and guidance.

## 3. Why These Problems Occur
- Information overload
- Limited trust signals
- Unstructured feedback
- Fragmented trip discovery

## 4. What Existing Sources Show
The source set identifies recognized concerns around fake or suspicious listings, booking safety, property/location verification and review integrity.

## 5. Consolidated Problem Statement
> Travelers need a more connected way to choose accommodation with confidence and relevance. They need to identify a stay that fits their personal needs, assess whether the property can be trusted, make sense of useful review information, and continue discovering the destination and local guidance without breaking the journey into disconnected tasks.

## 6. What Vistara Aims to Address
- **Stay** — find and compare suitable accommodation
- **Smart Recommendation** — rank suitable stays and explain why they match
- **Property Verification** — support stay/property verification with photo/property analysis
- **Explore** — continue with places, restaurants, experiences and local guidance

## 7. Scope Boundary
**In scope:** Stay + Smart Recommendation + Property Verification + Explore

**Out of scope:** Guest identity-upload verification

## 8. Research Limitation & Next Step
This statement defines what the prototype is designed to investigate; it does not claim that every traveler experiences every issue equally.

**Next step:** validate which problems matter most to intended users through user research.
