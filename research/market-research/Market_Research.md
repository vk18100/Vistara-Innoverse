# Vistara INNOVERSE — Market Research

## Research status
This document uses official tourism statistics and industry research. Commercial market estimates are identified as estimates. Vistara conclusions are research inferences, not measured market facts.

## 1. Research Objective
Understand India's travel/accommodation opportunity, digital traveler behavior and market conditions supporting Vistara's concept: **Stay + Smart Recommendation + Property Verification + Explore**.

## 2. India Tourism at a Glance
| Indicator | Latest figure | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic tourist visits | 2,948.19M in 2024; +17.51% vs 2023 | Large domestic travel base |
| Domestic visitor spending | US$203B in 2025 (WTTC) | Domestic travel dominates spending |
| Travel & Tourism contribution | US$263.6B; 46.2M jobs in 2025 (WTTC) | Large economic sector |
| Hotel industry | 9–12% revenue growth expected FY26 (ICRA via IBEF) | Active accommodation demand |
| Mobile online travel | 65.37% of bookings in 2025 (Mordor estimate) | Mobile-first UX matters |

## 3. Market Direction
- Domestic travel remains central.
- Digital travel booking is established and online penetration is rising.
- Mobile is a major booking channel.
- Accommodation demand remains active.
- Hotel supply is expanding beyond the largest metros.
- Travel platforms increasingly combine accommodation with attractions, restaurants, activities and experiences.

## 4. Traveler Behavior and Opportunity Signals
| Signal | Evidence | Vistara implication |
|---|---|---|
| Personalization | Airbnb/YouGov: 95% said trips should feel personal and unique; 92% said destination/stay should reflect personal taste. | Personalized recommendations |
| Accommodation importance | 82% said accommodation was very/extremely important. | Focus on stay selection |
| Discovery | 90% likely to seek places not widely recommended/viral. | Useful Explore |
| AI assistance | Booking.com 2026 predictions report strong openness to AI-powered suggestions. | AI-assisted discovery |
| AI trust | Booking.com 2025: 89% want AI in future travel planning; 6% fully trusted AI; 12% comfortable with AI deciding independently. | Explainability + control |

## 5. Relevant Market Segments
Budget/student travelers; leisure travelers; first-time visitors; frequent travelers; Tier-II/Tier-III destination travelers.

## 6. Market Implications
1. Reduce decision effort with transparent smart matching.
2. Put property verification near the stay decision.
3. Explain why AI recommends a property.
4. Connect the trip through Explore.

## 7. Market Gaps Relevant to the Prototype
- **Choice overload:** large inventories → transparent smart match.
- **Trust at decision time:** credibility signals → property verification.
- **Black-box recommendations:** limited autonomous AI trust → visible reasons and controls.
- **Fragmented planning:** separate travel services → Explore connects the stay to nearby options.
- **Local guidance discoverability:** separate content → keep Local Guidance inside Explore.

## 8. Why the Market Supports Vistara
Large domestic travel base, established digital/mobile booking, active accommodation demand, interest in personalization/discovery, and continued importance of trust create a credible market context for the prototype.

## 9. Market Opportunity Statement
**Build a mobile-friendly travel experience that helps users choose a suitable stay, understand why it is recommended, see the property's verification status, and continue into destination discovery.**

## 10. Limitations
Market-size figures differ in definition and methodology. Use them as directional ideathon evidence rather than a precise Vistara revenue forecast.
