# Vistara INNOVERSE — Competitor Analysis

## Research status
Based on current public information from official competitor pages. This document describes capabilities and identifies implications for Vistara; it does not rank competitors.

## 1. Research Objective
Study how established travel platforms handle accommodation search, trust, reviews, destination discovery and personalization, then identify a focused product space that Vistara can demonstrate without reproducing every feature.

## 2. Competitors Reviewed
Airbnb, Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, OYO.

## 3. Comparison Snapshot
| Platform | Documented capabilities | Implication for Vistara |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb | Search/ranking, many stay types, Experiences/Services, verified listings and identity/trust controls. | Connect search relevance, trust and destination experiences. |
| Booking.com | Broad accommodation discovery, verified guest reviews, Genius benefits, attractions and restaurant reservations. | Use verified-review signals and keep destination services close to stays. |
| MakeMyTrip | Hotels/homestays with filters; Tours & Attractions across tickets, tours, food, transport and more. | Make Explore useful beyond accommodation. |
| OYO | Hotels/homes, standardized/serviced stays, destination content and booking/payment safety guidance. | Make stay-quality and booking-safety cues visible. |

## 4. Platform Findings
### Airbnb
Public pages document search filters, an ordering algorithm using factors including quality, popularity, price and location, Experiences/Services, verified listings and review-fraud detection.

### Booking.com
Public pages document verified reviews from booked guests, review-fraud detection, Genius benefits, attractions and restaurant reservations.

### MakeMyTrip
Public pages document hotel/homestay filters and a Tours & Attractions platform covering attraction tickets, guided tours, adventure, food, transport and other activities.

### OYO
Public pages emphasize standardized/serviced stays, value, official payment channels and destination-oriented Discover content.

## 5. Gap Analysis
- Stay choice: transparent personalized match with reasons.
- Trust: property verification in the stay-decision flow.
- Review understanding: aspect-level review intelligence.
- Destination discovery: connect Explore to the chosen stay.
- Local guidance: keep it inside Explore.
- AI trust: show why a recommendation appears.

## 6. Vistara Positioning
**Stay → Smart Match → Verify → Explore**

## 7. What We Should Not Claim
- Vistara is not claimed to be the first platform to use these individual capabilities.
- Do not claim a competitor lacks a feature without evidence.
- Recommendation score is not a probability until trained/calibrated/evaluated.
- Guest identity-upload verification is not part of the competition prototype.

## 8. Locked Prototype Focus
**Stay + Smart Recommendation + Property Verification + Explore**
