# Vistara INNOVERSE — User Research

## Research status
This document combines secondary research with a primary-research plan. It does **not** claim that a Vistara user survey or interview has already been completed.

## 1. Research objective
Understand what travelers need when choosing a stay and when deciding what to do at the destination, then identify which Vistara features should be validated before implementation.

## 2. What existing research suggests
- **Airbnb / YouGov (2026):** 2,012 Gen Z respondents aged 18–29 across 11 Indian cities; 95% said trips should feel personal and unique, while 92% said the destination or stay should reflect personal taste.
- **Airbnb / YouGov (2026):** 82% considered accommodation very or extremely important when planning a trip, and 90% were likely to seek places not widely recommended or viral.
- **Booking.com (2025):** Its 2026 Travel Predictions, based on 29,733 travelers across 33 markets, describes growing demand for personalized travel and openness to AI-powered suggestions.
- **Booking.com (2025):** Its Global AI Sentiment Report found 89% wanted to use AI in future travel planning, while only 6% fully trusted AI and 12% were comfortable with AI making decisions independently.
- **Government of India (2025–2026):** I4C warned about online booking scams involving fake websites and tourist-service bookings; the Ministry of Tourism also reports complaints involving service deficiencies, overcharging and misleading packages.

## 3. Initial user groups
These are hypotheses, not validated personas.

| User group | Likely need | Question to validate |
|---|---|---|
| Young / student travelers | Budget, convenience, short trips and discovery | Which factors actually decide the stay? |
| Small groups / friends | Shared needs, location, suitable rooms and activities | How do groups compromise? |
| First-time visitors | Trust, reviews and destination guidance | What makes a property trustworthy? |
| Frequent travelers | Speed, personalization and relevant results | Which preferences should Vistara remember? |

## 4. Key needs to validate
- **Fit:** location, budget, dates, guests and amenities.
- **Trust:** credible property details and photos.
- **Clarity:** understandable recommendation reasons.
- **Discovery:** places, food, experiences and local guidance.
- **Control:** understand and adjust AI-assisted results.

## 5. User research questions
1. What are the top three factors you consider before booking a stay?
2. How often do you compare multiple properties before choosing one?
3. What information makes you trust or distrust a property listing?
4. How useful are reviews when deciding between similar stays?
5. Have you ever found that a property looked different from its online photos or details?
6. Would recommendations based on your budget, location, amenities and preferences be useful?
7. When a system recommends a stay, do you want to know why it was recommended?
8. What would make you comfortable or uncomfortable using AI for travel decisions?
9. After selecting a stay, what destination information do you still need?
10. Would Places, Restaurants, Experiences and Local Guidance in one Explore section be useful?

## 6. Primary research plan
| Method | Suggested sample | What to learn |
|---|---:|---|
| Online survey | 15–30 target users | Booking factors, trust, AI/recommendation preferences and Explore needs |
| Short interviews | 5–8 users | Decision process, confusing points, trust concerns and desired experience |
| Prototype test | 5 users | Search → Recommendation → Property → Explore usability |

## 7. Research-to-product mapping
| Finding to validate | Potential Vistara response | What to measure |
|---|---|---|
| Many stays can make selection difficult | Smart recommendation and ranking | Selection time and relevance feedback |
| Users need stronger trust signals | Property verification workflow | Perceived usefulness of verification |
| Users may not want black-box AI decisions | Explainable recommendations | Understanding and acceptance of reasons |
| Users need more than accommodation information | Explore | Explore usage and task completion |

## 8. Expected user journey
**Search → Compare → Decide → Verify → Explore**

## 9. Research decisions for the prototype
- Treat secondary findings as hypotheses until primary user research is completed.
- Use recommendation as decision support with visible reasons and user control.
- Property verification is for the stay/property, not guest identity upload.
- Local Guidance stays inside Explore.
- Use research to prioritize the prototype.

## 10. Limitations
The evidence is secondary research and government advisories. Direct Vistara user research is still required for validation.
